## Creative boundary

The game will be an original cubic dark-park experience inspired only by the public high-level premise: a mysterious park, a house of celebrity culture, a clown-like atmosphere, exploration, and puzzles. It will not use real people as characters, copied channel art, logos, screenshots, Telegram posts, or claims about the channel owners.
